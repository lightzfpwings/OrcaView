# ORCA log coverage

OrcaView 0.1.0a61 displays per-atom electron populations, spin populations and charges from Mulliken, Löwdin, Hirshfeld and MBIS analyses. CHELPG supplies fitted charges. Mulliken gross populations are read from the Mayer table’s NA column when available. For charge-only columns of a density partition, the conversion uses the nuclear charge ZA from the verified coordinate or Mayer table minus the printed atomic charge. This excludes electrons represented by an effective core potential. Values retain the source lines and atom identities of the reported geometry. The OH doublet example comes from a local ORCA 6.1.1 calculation. Its host name and working directory have been omitted. [OH output](../examples/populations/hydroxyl.out) · [ORCA population analysis](https://orca-manual.mpi-muelheim.mpg.de/contents/spectroscopyproperties/population.html)

In November 2026, the OrcaView results importer was run on 17 completed ORCA 6.1.1 calculations from the project validation set. Public and local copies of the same calculation were counted once. The set includes single points, a compound job, optimization, correlated methods, solvent models, frequency calculations, electronic spectra and NMR. The importer produced 34 distinct scalar property labels across these jobs.

| Area | Available in Results | Additional data printed in these logs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy and orbitals | SCF and final energies, selected correlation energies, T1 diagnostic, HOMO, LUMO and gap. Repeated values retain their job or optimization step. | All 17 logs print orbital energies and occupations beyond the frontier orbitals. |
| Geometry and bonding | A supported coordinate block with atom identities, per-atom Mulliken, Löwdin and Hirshfeld populations when available. | All 17 print Mayer bond orders. Three print Cartesian gradients. The optimization log also gives step-by-step RMS and maximum gradient and displacement against their thresholds. |
| Response and thermochemistry | Dipole vector and magnitude, static polarizability tensor, rotational constants, ZPE, U, H, T·S, G and selected solvation terms. | The thermochemistry sections also print individual translational, rotational and vibrational contributions. |
| Vibrations and electronic excitations | Mode frequencies, IR intensities, Raman activities, UV/visible oscillator strengths and signed CD rotatory strengths. | Eight logs contain normal-mode displacement vectors. The TD-DFT output identifies contributing orbital excitations and their weights for individual states. |
| NMR | Isotropic shielding and anisotropy by nucleus, an uncoupled one-dimensional plot for each element, optional reference-derived shifts, and signed J values by pair. | The shielding calculation prints full per-nucleus tensors. A coupled spectral simulation also requires spin-system and spectrometer information. |

The water Raman output contains the orbital table at line 526, Mayer bonds at 637, the Cartesian gradient at 678 and normal modes at 942. The first geometry-convergence table in the optimization output starts at line 960. These sections can be read from the text log itself.
